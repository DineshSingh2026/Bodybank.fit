Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 May 2026

This policy explains what personal information BodyBank collects when you use our website, mobile app, and related services, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights over it. It applies to body, health, fitness, and account data — including photos and measurements you upload.

1. Who we are

BodyBank ("BodyBank", "we", "us", "our") provides a lifestyle, training, nutrition, and body-tracking platform available at www.bodybank.fit and as the BodyBank mobile application on Google Play.

For privacy questions, data access requests, or to exercise your rights, contact us at: support@bodybank.fit.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Password (stored only as a salted hash — we never see your plaintext password)
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account email, name, and profile image, via Google OAuth

Body, health and fitness data (sensitive)

  • Body photos and progress photos you upload
  • Body measurements such as weight, height, body composition, body-fat percentage, muscle measurements
  • Body scans and AI-generated body analysis
  • Training programs, workout logs, exercise progress
  • Nutrition logs, meal records, calorie and macro data
  • Goals, lifestyle inputs, and self-reported wellbeing information
Sensitive data. Body, health, and fitness information is treated as sensitive personal data under applicable laws (including India's DPDPA and the EU GDPR). We process it only with your consent and only to provide the BodyBank service to you.

Communication data

  • Messages and posts you choose to share to public or community feeds
  • Email, SMS, and in-app notifications we send you
  • Support tickets or correspondence you send us

Device, technical and usage data

  • Device model, operating system, app version, language, time zone
  • IP address, approximate location derived from IP, network type
  • App and page usage events, feature interactions, crash logs
  • Web push notification subscription identifiers (if you opt in)

Payment data (when applicable)

At launch the BodyBank app is free to use. When paid plans or in-app purchases are added in the future, we will not store full card or banking details. Payments will be processed by a regulated payment processor that returns only a tokenized identifier and the transaction status to us.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account and authenticate you on each session
  • To provide the core BodyBank service: training plans, nutrition tracking, body scans, progress reports, community feed
  • To generate AI-driven body analysis, recommendations, and program adjustments based on data you submit
  • To deliver transactional messages by email, SMS, web push, or in-app notification (e.g. account verification, password reset, reminders you have opted into)
  • To improve product quality, debug issues, monitor abuse, and maintain security
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your body or health data to train third-party advertising profiles.

4. Legal bases for processing

Depending on your jurisdiction, we rely on one or more of: your consent (especially for body/health data and notifications), performance of the contract between you and BodyBank, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, and compliance with legal obligations.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or irreversibly anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain certain records (e.g. tax, fraud-prevention) for a longer period. Backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule and will not exceed 90 days.

7. Your rights

Subject to local law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and personal data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, including for marketing or notifications
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority in your country

To exercise any of these rights, email support@bodybank.fit. You can also delete your account directly from the app under Settings → Delete account.

8. Security

BodyBank uses industry-standard security measures including HTTPS / TLS for all data in transit, password hashing with bcrypt, signed JSON Web Tokens for sessions, role-based access controls for staff, encrypted storage of media files, and regular dependency and infrastructure reviews. No system is perfectly secure, but we work continuously to protect your information.

9. Children

BodyBank is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

BodyBank operates globally. Your data may be processed in countries other than your own (including India and the United States, where some of our service providers are located). Where required, we use contractual safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) to protect your data during these transfers.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

The BodyBank website uses cookies and local storage to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and measure aggregate analytics. The mobile app uses the platform's secure local storage for the same purposes. You can clear these at any time from your browser or device settings.

12. Mobile app permissions

The BodyBank Android app requests permissions only as needed:

  • Internet: to connect to our servers (required)
  • Camera / Photos (only when you upload a body or progress photo)
  • Notifications (optional, only after you accept the system prompt)

The app does not collect contacts, microphone audio, SMS messages, or precise GPS location.

13. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
