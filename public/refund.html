Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy covers what you pay for online at BodyBank: the membership plans (Core, Guided and Tribe Elite) and BloodMap, our one-time blood report and consultation service. It explains when you can get your money back, how to ask, and how long it takes. Sections 1 to 4 are about membership plans. Section 5 is about BloodMap, which has its own refund rule. The rest applies to both.

In short, for membership plans. Every new member starts with a free trial, so you can try BodyBank before paying. If you pay and change your mind, ask within 7 days of the payment and we refund the full amount. After 7 days a plan is not refundable, except where we charged you by mistake or could not provide the service.

1. How plans work

  • Each plan is a one-time payment for a fixed term (for example 1, 4 or 12 months). It gives you access to that plan's features until the term ends.
  • Plans do not renew automatically. We never charge your card or UPI again without you making a new payment yourself.
  • The price shown at checkout is the full amount you pay. Nothing is added on top of it.

2. When you can get a refund

  • Within 7 days of a payment: ask for a refund for any reason and we refund the full amount of that payment. This applies to a first purchase and to a renewal.
  • Charged twice, or charged the wrong amount: we refund the extra amount in full, at any time.
  • Paid, but your plan did not unlock: tell us and we will activate it. If we cannot, we refund the payment in full.
  • We cannot provide the service: if we stop offering your plan or cannot deliver it, we refund the unused part of your term.

3. When a refund does not apply

  • More than 7 days have passed since the payment, other than the cases listed in section 2.
  • You did not use the app or your coaching during a term you paid for.
  • Your account was suspended or closed for breaking our Terms of Service.
  • The free trial: it costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund.

4. Cancelling a plan

Because plans do not renew by themselves, there is no subscription to cancel. You can stop using BodyBank at any time. If you stop part-way through a term, your access continues until the term ends and the remaining time is not refunded, except as set out in section 2.

You can delete your account at any time from the app under Settings → Delete account. Deleting your account does not by itself create a refund.

5. BloodMap by BodyBank

BloodMap is a one-time payment for three things: a Health Map report prepared from the blood report you upload, a consultation with a doctor, and a consultation with a sports nutritionist. The 7-day rule in section 2 does not apply to BloodMap. These rules apply instead.

  • Before you upload your blood report: ask for a refund for any reason and we refund the full amount.
  • After you upload your blood report: our work on it starts straight away, so the payment can no longer be refunded because you changed your mind.
  • We could not read your report: we ask you for a clearer copy. If you cannot provide one, we refund the full amount.
  • We could not deliver: if we do not deliver your Health Map report, we refund the full amount. If the report was delivered but we cannot provide a consultation, we refund the part of the price that covers that consultation.
  • Charged twice, or charged the wrong amount: we refund the extra amount in full, at any time.
  • Missed consultations: you can move a consultation to another time free of charge. A consultation you booked and did not attend is not refunded.
  • What a full refund does: it closes the BloodMap order. The report, the private link to it and any booked consultations are no longer available.

6. How to ask for a refund

  1. Email support@bodybank.fit or message us on WhatsApp at +91 95025 75669.
  2. Tell us the email address on your BodyBank account or BloodMap order and the date of the payment. The payment id from your receipt helps us find it faster.
  3. We reply within 2 working days to confirm whether the refund is approved.

7. How and when you get the money

  • An approved refund is sent back to the same payment method you paid with (the same card, UPI account, bank account or wallet). We cannot refund to a different account.
  • We start the refund within 2 working days of approving it. It usually reaches you in 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank or card issuer.
  • When a payment is refunded in full, the plan it paid for ends. Your account, history and past reports stay with you.

8. Failed payments

If a payment fails but money left your account, your bank normally returns it automatically within 5 to 7 working days. No plan is activated for a failed payment. If the money has not come back after 7 working days, contact us with the payment details and we will follow it up with our payment partner.

9. Delivery

BodyBank is a digital service. Nothing is shipped. Your plan is activated on your account as soon as the payment is confirmed, usually within a few seconds, and is available straight away on the website and in the BodyBank app.

For BloodMap, your Health Map report is delivered on your private order page, normally within 48 hours of uploading your blood report, and we email you when it is ready. Consultations take place by phone or WhatsApp call at the times you book.
