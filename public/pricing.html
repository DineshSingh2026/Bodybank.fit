    Questions

    How does the free trial work?

    Sign up and you get instant access for 7 days, with no card needed. Before it ends, your coach will reach out to set up the plan that suits you.

    Why 4 months for Guided?

    Four months is one full blood-report cycle. You test at the start, work the plan with your coach, then retest to see what actually changed in your markers.

    Can I change plans later?

    Yes. Move up or down at any time and your coach will update your plan. Your history, streaks and past reports stay with you.

    What happens to my data if I move to Core?

    Nothing is deleted. Reports you already have stay readable. Guided and Tribe Elite features pause until you move back up.

    How do I pay?

    Your coach sets up your plan with you, and your app unlocks automatically once it is active.

    Can I get a refund?

    Yes. Ask within 7 days of a payment and we refund it in full, back to the method you paid with. Plans do not renew automatically, so you are never charged again without paying yourself. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

    I already have time left on my plan. What happens if I pay now?

    If you buy the plan you are already on, the new term is added on top of the days you have left. If you move to a different plan, the new plan starts today.

    Not sure which plan fits?

    Tell us your goal and we'll recommend one. No pressure.
