Coach Mode exercise source data and photos
==========================================

Exercise records and the start/end photos used by Coach Mode come from
free-exercise-db by yuhonas: https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db

That project is released under the Unlicense (public domain dedication):
https://unlicense.org — no attribution is required, but BodyBank credits it
anyway in the Coach Mode "About" line.

BodyBank rewrites the steps, cues and common mistakes (data/coach/drafts/),
and every exercise is reviewed by a coach before members can see it.
Photos are re-encoded to WebP (480w / 960w) with metadata stripped.
