Questions

Which blood reports can I upload?

Any blood test report from a lab in India or abroad: full body checkups, thyroid, lipid, liver, kidney, sugar, vitamins and more. A PDF from the lab works best. Clear photos of each page work too.

How old can the report be?

For useful advice, use a report from the last 3 months. Older reports still work, but your body may have changed since.

I do not have a blood report yet. What should I do?

Get a test done at any lab near you first. If you are not sure which tests to ask for, message us on WhatsApp and we will suggest a panel.

When do I get my report?

Within 48 hours of uploading. We message you as soon as it is ready, so you do not need to keep this page open.

How do the calls work?

After you upload, you pick a time for the doctor call and then the sports nutritionist call. At that time we call you on the mobile number you gave. You can change a slot up to 12 hours before it starts.

Do I need to install the BodyBank app?

No. Everything happens on this page. You get a private link by email and WhatsApp to come back to your order at any time.

Is my health data safe?

Your report is only seen by our review team and the experts on your calls. Your order opens only with your private link, or a one-time code sent to your own email and mobile.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in full, if you ask before we start analysing your report. Once your report has been prepared, the service has been delivered. See the Refund Policy.
